Can you teach better than an AI?

Aino is 10 and learning fractions: six ideas, each building on the one before. You get 30 turns over Monday, Wednesday and Friday. The exam is next Monday. You only see the answers.

The rules

The AI got exactly these rules. Sources added for you.
  • Answers are noisy. A child who knows an idea answers right about 9 times in 10. A child who doesn’t still guesses right about 1 time in 4.Bayesian knowledge tracing (Corbett & Anderson 1995)
  • Ideas build on each other. An idea is very hard to learn until the one before it is learned.Knowledge space theory (Doignon & Falmagne 1985)
  • Teaching may not click. Each explanation has some chance of working; it differs by child and idea.Knowledge tracing learn rate (Corbett & Anderson 1995)
  • Repeating in one session helps less. Explaining the same idea again in the same session works less each time; in a later session it works as well as the first time.Spacing effect (Cepeda et al. 2006)
  • Memory fades. Something learned can be forgotten within days unless it is reviewed.Forgetting curves; FSRS, fitted on millions of real reviews
  • Quizzing helps memory. A quiz strengthens memory more than another explanation, especially after some time has passed. But a quiz never teaches an idea that wasn’t learned.Testing effect (Roediger & Karpicke 2006)
  • Some children learn a wrong rule. It shows as a typical wrong answer: 1/8 > 1/4 (idea 2), 2/3 = 3/4 (idea 3), 1/2 + 1/3 = 2/5 (idea 5, also shows in idea 6). “Fix” works on it; re-explaining rarely does.These errors are documented in fraction learning (Ni & Zhou 2005; Brown & Burton 1978). The fix rates are our settings.
  • The child may already know some early ideas.
  • The exam counts all six ideas equally: known and remembered ≈ 90%, not known ≈ 25% (guessing), a wrong rule ≈ 10%.

The sources support each effect. The exact numbers (such as 9 in 10, 1 in 4, the exam scores) are our settings, listed in the project’s REFERENCES.md; the result holds across 27 variations of the key numbers. Forgetting speeds come from FSRS defaults.
